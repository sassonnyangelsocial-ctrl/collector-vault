# Collector Vault Free-Forever Launch

## Launch position

Collector Vault is now free for every collector. No card is required to create a vault, browse the complete catalog, track a collection, organize missing figures, manage quantities and duplicates, build wishlist/ISO/DISO/trade lists, share social-ready collection images, or export collection data.

Collector Vault Pro is the optional power tier for collectors, live hosts, and sellers. It includes reciprocal Trade Match and private chat, verified alerts and release calendars, Live Wheel hosting, Seller Pro, Whatnot CSV importing, and advanced analytics and automation as those features launch. Pro is $4.99 monthly or $49.99 yearly after a one-time 7-day trial.

Guest viewers can join a shared Live Wheel room free without a membership. Hosting and controlling a Live Wheel requires Pro.
